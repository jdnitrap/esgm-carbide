Continues the same 2026-09-13/14 session as [[esgr-growth-and-modulation-2026-09]] and [[esgr-trained-head-and-kwta-fix-2026-09]] (read those first). Committed as esgr repo commit `5f291d2`, following `288845e` (heavy training + ROLE_MAP close-out).

**1. Autoregressive generation is real now (`generate_bytes.py`).** The trained head (`NextByteRNN`) previously only ran teacher-forced; it can now sample its own next byte and feed it back in, querying the graph live at each step for structural tags (`word_at_position()`/`build_tag_table()` were already causal-only, so this needed zero changes to be streaming-safe). Verified rigorously, not just "it runs": 93.7% of generated tokens are valid English words, 100% of generated role-transitions matched a role-bigram that genuinely occurs in the real corpus (mined from 262,485 real adjacent pairs), stable over 1000-byte runs with no repetition collapse, 88.5% word-validity even on seed words verified absent from the entire training corpus (real generalization, not just memorization — though ~67% of 4-word windows ARE verbatim corpus recall, so it leans heavily on memorized phrasing too). 0 frozen edges ever touched.

**2. Two new deterministic "speech mechanics" columns in `head.py`**, same hand-given-fact pattern as `byte_identity.py`: `TURN` (QUESTION/ANSWER/none, a pure forward scan for "Q:"/"A:" markers) and `QUESTION_FORM` (WH_WHAT/WH_WHO/WH_WHERE/WH_WHEN/WH_WHY/WH_HOW/WH_WHICH/YES_NO/IMPERATIVE, detected from each question's real opening word and **held through its answer**, not reset at "A:", so the signal is live exactly when generating the answer needs it). N_COLUMNS: 32 → 35 (TURN) → 45 (QUESTION_FORM).

**3. Real discourse relations, grounded in published research (Penn Discourse Treebank), not invented** — user explicitly asked me to search for prior art rather than guess. `grammar_extra.py`'s `DISCOURSE` dimension extended from 3 values (AFFIRM/NEGATE/NEGATOR) to 7, adding PDTB's standard four semantic classes: TEMPORAL, CONTINGENCY, COMPARISON, EXPANSION. 28 real connective words wired in (because/since/so/therefore→CONTINGENCY, but/however/although→COMPARISON, and/also/moreover→EXPANSION, then/after/before→TEMPORAL), 22 newly tiled as real graph words. N_COLUMNS 45→49.

**4. Real self-dimension-discovery (`discover_dimension.py`)** — the one capability genuinely new in kind, not just another hand-given column. Explicit user request: "make it have the ability to add dimension to itself." Uses the distributional hypothesis (real, citable NLP technique): clusters currently-UNassigned words by the ROLE of their real preceding/following neighbor word, mined from the actual corpus. Human-gated exactly like `fact_gate.py` — `propose_dimensions()` is read-only, `confirm_dimension()` is the only thing that writes, called explicitly. Found and confirmed two real clusters against the live graph: `DISCOVERED_ADJECTIVE_LIKE` (only/present/predetermined/examined/double) and `DISCOVERED_NOUN_LIKE` (study/search/best/worst/shadow) — both correctly rediscovered real grammatical categories from pure context statistics, no ROLE_MAP hint given. **Not yet wired into head.py's N_COLUMNS/build_tag_table** — discovered dimensions are confirmed in the graph and in `discovered_dimensions.json`, but the trained head doesn't read them yet; that's a real next step if this gets picked up again.

**5. Six rounds of dialogue fine-tuning, all honestly reported as failures to reach real conversational coherence — a genuine, diagnosed ceiling, not swept under the rug:**
- Round 1: 51 pairs × 6 repeats × 200 epochs → catastrophic overfitting (held-out accuracy dead flat 84.05%→84.03%, essay fluency dropped 96.3%→86.6%)
- Round 2: 203 pairs (66 facts × 3 phrasings) + TURN column → overfitting collapse fixed, but content still topically irrelevant to the question asked
- Round 2.5: + QUESTION_FORM column → no improvement; confirmed this was a DATA problem, not a missing-signal problem
- Round 3 (827 pairs, 137 facts × 6 phrasings, 40 epochs) → healthier accuracy curve but worse output (still climbing, needed more budget)
- Round 3 heavy (150 epochs) → accuracy plateaued cleanly (76.2%→82.7%) but **real mode collapse**: identical short garbled answers ("Pater. Theshs, better world.") for different questions
- Round 4 (+ PDTB discourse relations + discovered dimensions, N_COLUMNS→49, 40 epochs) → held-out accuracy started at just 16% (vs ~77-84% every prior warm-start) because 22 newly-wired connective words are among the most common in English, so this one column change shifted the training-data tag distribution far more than any earlier addition; climbed to 78% but still visibly under-converged, and conversation quality was **worse**, not better, with the essay-only baseline checkpoint (never retrained on the new columns) breaking down completely ("f cs , Do fe, sio,, Do fe, sio,...").

**Honest final diagnosis, stated directly to the user rather than kept ambiguous:** every structural piece (TURN, QUESTION_FORM, PDTB discourse, discovered dimensions) is real, correctly wired, and verified safe (0 frozen edges disturbed across the entire day, full test suite green after every change). But genuine conversational coherence never improved through any of 6 attempts regardless of data volume, phrasing variety, epoch count, or added structural columns — strong evidence this is a **real data-scale ceiling** (a few hundred KB of dialogue text and a small single-layer GRU), not something more architecture or more training fixes. [[feedback_tune_against_real_data]] was followed throughout (every hyperparameter change was tested against real held-out numbers, not assumed).

**For a future session:** don't just re-run more epochs on `dialogue_corpus.txt` expecting different results — that lever is exhausted (tried at 12, 40, 150, and 200 epochs, across 3 dataset sizes, same collapse pattern every time). If dialogue capability gets picked up again, the real next moves are: (a) wire `discovered_dimensions.json` into `head.py`'s `N_COLUMNS`/`build_tag_table` (never done), (b) retrain `head_checkpoint.pt` itself (the plain essay-only checkpoint) now that the graph has grown to 49 columns — it currently breaks down badly if loaded fresh since it's never seen the new high-frequency DISCOURSE columns, (c) consider chain-of-thought-style answers (visible reasoning steps in the training text) rather than one-line facts, since "reasoning" was explicitly ruled out as a hand-wireable column (no deterministic function computes "the right answer" the way `is_alpha` or `TURN` can — it has to be genuinely learned from data that demonstrates it).

**Final diagnosis, reached collaboratively at the very end of the session (worth reading if [[esgr-trained-head-and-kwta-fix-2026-09]]'s open items get picked up), and it changes the priority of (a)-(c) above:** the real root cause was never epoch count — it's that `dialogue_corpus.txt` is short, formulaic, one-sentence-per-answer, same rhythm every time. The essay-only head is fluent BECAUSE the essay corpus is rich, varied, natural prose; the dialogue data never had that same quality bar. The user drew a direct, correct analogy to how instruction-tuning actually works in real LLMs (asked "how were you trained" and reasoned from the answer): pretraining on broad rich text produces fluent continuation (already achieved here); a separate fine-tuning stage on real prompt→response *demonstrations*, written to the SAME quality bar as the pretraining data, is what teaches conversation — not just more examples in a thin shape. Also confirmed directly: held-out next-BYTE accuracy is NOT a reliable proxy for conversational coherence at this scale — round 5 (150 epochs) had accuracy climb cleanly to 82.7% while output degraded into repeated garbage ("Pater. Theshs, better world." for multiple different questions). **So the highest-priority next step, above (a)-(c), is: rewrite `dialogue_corpus.txt` with genuinely rich, natural, multi-sentence answers instead of one-line facts — do NOT just add more pairs in the same thin shape, that specific lever (quantity) is exhausted and proven not to work; quality/richness of each example was never actually tried.**
